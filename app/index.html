The grey band is Hyperliquid's own best bid and offer. The blue band is where this desk is willing to trade.

When nothing is happening the blue band sits outside the grey one on both sides: the desk is never the best price on the screen, so a stale quote is not worth taking.

When the book breaks, one side of the blue band crosses inside the grey one and the wedge lights up — amber when the desk is buying from forced sellers, teal when it is selling to forced buyers. That is the desk stepping in front of L1 to take the flow, on one side only, and only while the dislocation lasts.

Spot is real Coinbase data from the 2025-10-10 cascade; the desk's own two prices are CoreQuote.bounds() answering under each minute's book. The book, the forced flow and both takers in that file are still synthetic, so the inventory, PnL and markout numbers show the shape of the comparison and not its size. The two markout series sit within twenty basis points of each other for a real reason and a placeholder one: leaning in means paying up, so the desk's entry price on a fill is slightly worse than the control's, and its case is the size it absorbs rather than the price of any one fill — which is exactly what the placeholder takers do not model, since they hand both makers nearly the same flow. results/oct10_replay.schema.md says which layer is which.
